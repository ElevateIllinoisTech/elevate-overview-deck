# Elevate Overview Deck

A 10-slide, Illinois Tech–branded overview of Elevate, Illinois Tech's career-readiness pathway, written for students, families and partners who are new to it.

**Live:** https://elevateillinoistech.github.io/elevate-overview-deck/

## Presenting
- Arrow keys, Space or swipe to move between slides; `#3` at the end of the link opens slide 3.
- **N** shows the speaker notes for the current slide.
- **F** goes full screen.
- Print or Save as PDF from the browser gives one slide per page.

## Editing
- **Quick text edits in the browser:** press **E** (or click Edit), click any heading or line and type, then click **Save edited copy**. That downloads a new `index.html`. Upload it to this repo to replace the old one (Add file › Upload files), and the live link updates in about a minute.
- **On GitHub:** open `index.html`, click the pencil icon, edit the text between the tags, and commit. Each slide is one `<section id="…">` block in order: cover, what, who, acronym, cycle, experiences, badges, support, gain, start. Speaker notes are the `<aside>` at the end of each section.
- **Look and colours:** the shared styles are in the `<style>` block at the top. Brand colours: scarlet `#CC0000`, near-black `#161414`, warm white `#F7F5F2`.

## Assets
`assets/` holds the Illinois Tech Elevate lockups (red box with white or black "Elevate"), the black-and-white photo of S. R. Crown Hall, and the "Scan to enter the Elevate Universe" QR code (elevate.iit.edu).

## Wording rules
Elevate is a **pathway**, never a "program". Always "Elevate Success Coach".
